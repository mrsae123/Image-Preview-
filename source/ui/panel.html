How Image Preview works
Preview & pin images

Paste an image link or a webpage link, then tap Preview. Pin on chat keeps it floating over the chat so you can look at it while you type. Drag to move, pinch or use +/- to resize. The pin's toolbar also has reset, rotate, flip, fullscreen and remove; tap ︿ to fold the toolbar away. Clear all next to "Pinned on chat" unpins everything at once — it only unpins them, nothing is deleted. If the floating button ends up stuck off-screen, use "Reset floating button position" in the chat's right sidebar (Plugin · Image Preview) to bring it back. You can pin up to 30 images at once; remove one to pin another. Only images, GIFs and web pages are ever fetched.

Extract images from text

Got a big pasted character card or post full of pictures? Paste the whole thing into the chat input, then tap "Extract images from text" in the + menu — it quietly ignores all the text and hands you just the images and GIFs to look through. Everything starts checked; tap any tile you don't want to leave it out, then Save selected (or just Save all) tucks them into Saved Previews. Want a few of them floating on the chat too? Head to Saved Previews afterward and use Select. You can also skip the pasting: tap "Scan this chat for images" in the panel (or in the right sidebar) and it searches the whole chat for you — the character card, persona, lorebooks and every message, newest first. Tap Rescan chat any time after the chat changes and it tells you what's new. Images you've already saved are marked Saved and skipped. Scanning only reads the chat on your device; the pictures themselves load from their original links. Links to videos, music, archives, apps or other files are ignored.

Character page

Open it from the panel, or from "Character page" in the chat's right sidebar (Plugin · Image Preview). It reads the open chat's card (creator notes, description, personality and scenario) and shows it as one scrollable page, with the pictures and GIFs where the card put them, a bit like a bot's intro page. Tap a picture to see it bigger. In a group chat, tap a name at the top to switch characters. Bright text colours are kept; backgrounds are not. It is read on your device, and the pictures load from their original links only when you open the page. Opening it from the panel closes the panel; tap the floating button to bring the panel back.

Save & manage images

Save preview keeps a copy for this chat, and every pinned image is saved automatically too. Tap Select above the saved grid to pick a few and Pin selected — handy right after Extract images from text, or anytime you just fancy a couple back on the chat. Tap any saved image normally for Fullscreen, Pin, Save to device, Copy link, Rename, Info and Delete. Rename only changes the label; the image itself is never touched. Deleting one — or using Clear all — doesn't erase it right away either; see Recycle Bin below. The saved grid shows 200 images at a time; tap Show more at its end for the rest.

Recycle Bin

Nothing's gone the moment you delete it. Deleted saved images rest in the Recycle Bin first — 7 or 30 days, whichever you've picked under Settings — so a stray tap on Clear all is never the end of the world. Open it anytime from "Open Recycle Bin" in the chat's right sidebar, tick whatever you want, then Restore (it lands in the chat you have open) or Delete forever if you're sure. Anything you never touch quietly clears itself out once its own time is up.

Make it yours

See the little monkey munching a banana next to the title? Poke him — he gets grumpy, then opens the look options: a wallpaper from your phone (or an image link), how dark it is, a nature-inspired panel background like Forest or Aurora, the panel font, and your floating button's icon and color. Everything applies right away, and "Back to defaults" undoes your changes.

Quick Lookup ⚓

The ⚓ hangs on a rope and sways by itself. Pull it down to open Translate, Meaning and Web Search. To close, tap the ⚓ or pull it down again. A tap on the closed anchor only gives the rope a tug.

Game Corner

Tap 🎮 for a Jump Game, Snake and Memory Bomb, or add your own game. Drag the top bar to move the window and the corner to resize it. The ⚙ button inside a game holds your player name and that game's own settings and resets.

Your privacy

Saved images and pins belong only to this chat — the one exception is the Recycle Bin, which holds deleted images across every chat until they're restored or their time runs out. Built-in games are fully offline. Games you add yourself run in an isolated box and cannot read your chats or Tavo data. Privacy Mode blocks every lookup request. Nothing is collected or sent to the author. Only images, GIFs and web pages are downloaded; links to videos, apps, archives and other files are refused before anything is fetched. You can read the Terms of Use again below.

If something won't close or open

Every box closes with its × at the top, by tapping outside it, or with Close at the bottom (Help and Character page). If the floating button is off-screen, use "Reset floating button position" in the chat's right sidebar. If a pin says the limit is reached, remove one first. If a link won't preview, it may not be an image, GIF or web page, or the site may block outside apps.
